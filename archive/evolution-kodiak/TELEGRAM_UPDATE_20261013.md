🛠 **Evolution X update for the Pixel 11 series: HiLight settings and two fixes**

New builds for the **Pixel 11 Pro XL (kodiak)**, **Pixel 11 Pro (grizzly)**, **Pixel 11 (cubs)** and **Pixel 11 Pro Fold (yogi)**.

✨ **New**: HiLight settings in Settings > System > HiLight (Pro, Pro XL and Fold). Turn the light bar on the back on or off for Gemini and for calls from starred contacts, with a preview.
✅ **Fixed**: a bright flash when the screen times out into the always-on display in a dark room.
✅ **Fixed**: the PIN being asked twice after a reboot before fingerprint works. Google Play services was preparing Google's own updates, which can't install over this ROM; its system updater is now disabled.
Thanks to Ashray V K for the logs. Updating keeps your data: `fastboot update <zip>`
