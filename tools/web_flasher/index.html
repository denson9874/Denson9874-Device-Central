ExpressiveOS Web Flasher

Install ExpressiveOS (Android 17) directly to your Google Pixel 11 Pro / XL via WebUSB Fastboot in Google Chrome.

  1. Reboot Pixel 11 Pro / XL into Bootloader mode (Hold Power + Volume Down).
  2. Connect device via high-speed USB-C cable to your computer.
  3. Click Connect Device and select your Pixel device in the prompt.
  4. Click Flash ExpressiveOS to start the automated installation.
Ready. Connect device to begin.
