# Port LineageOS husky trees into DerpFest 17

There is no DerpFest 17 husky tree to start from. Every DerpFest-Devices husky repo (`device_google_shusky`, `device_google_zuma` and `vendor_google_husky`) stops at the Android 16 branch `16.2` ([shusky branches](https://api.github.com/repos/DerpFest-Devices/device_google_shusky/branches), [vendor commits](https://github.com/DerpFest-Devices/vendor_google_husky/commits/16.2)). The cleanest first build therefore uses **LineageOS lineage-24.0 `device/google/{husky,shusky,zuma,shusky-kernels}` plus TheMuppets lineage-24.0 `vendor/google/husky`**, pinned to the revisions LineageOS used for its successful official nightly of 2026-10-16 ([build-manifest.xml](https://mirrorbits.lineageos.org/full/husky/20261016/build-manifest.xml)). On top of that come about five small DerpFest-specific changes. The base is also what your Evolution X husky port already proved boots. It is newer and less modified than the Evolution-X-Devices cnb trees, which use Android 16 vendor blobs and pin `shusky-kernels` to lineage-23.2, and than crDroid 17.0, which uses a different kernel layout ([Evolution vendor cnb](https://github.com/Evolution-X-Devices/vendor_google_husky/commits/cnb), [crDroid kernel commit](https://github.com/crdroidandroid/android_device_google_shusky/commit/3679a4a02)). Most of your Evolution patch set does not carry over. Of the nine patches, three apply unchanged (E3 odex, E5 default-permissions rename, E8 gpuflag), one is replaced by a narrower DerpFest version (E6), one is replaced by DerpFest's own maintainer mechanism (E2), and four are dropped (E1, E4, E7, E9). The cause is the GMS package: DerpFest uses PixelOS `gms-17`, which suffixes its overlays and installs no Now Playing files, and DerpFest already turns off artifact-path enforcement globally. Two items have no Evolution counterpart. One is `PRODUCT_BUILD_IGNORE_APEX_CONTRIBUTION_CONTENTS := true`, which the GMS package makes necessary. The other is whether to keep Google's Class-3 face unlock or let DerpFest's ParanoidSense replace it. On maintainer status, husky is still listed as **Official/Active under Adrian Szymański (@Adi20_01)**, so any unofficial build must ship as `Community`. It also needs its own OTA JSON and update host. Because no phone is available, the release has to pass an offline gate covering image completeness, firmware version, SELinux domains for init services, VINTF, signatures, and a diff against the device-tested LineageOS nightly. It should be labelled as unbooted.

## Only LineageOS lineage-24.0 is a viable Android 17 base today

Every Android 17 husky tree found is a fork of Michael Bestas's LineageOS lineage-24.0 work with a thin ROM layer on top. The forks differ in how stale they are and how heavy the ROM layer is. **LineageOS** builds husky officially every week (maintainer mikeioannina). The latest nightly is `lineage-24.0-20261016`, and the lunch target is `lineage_husky-cp2a-userdebug` ([hudson targets](https://github.com/LineageOS/hudson/blob/main/lineage-build-targets), [download API](https://download.lineageos.org/api/v2/devices/husky/builds)). Its branch tips (shusky `21e0f82`, zuma `ab6deaa`, both 2026-10-16) add face-unlock overlays and a face permission XML on top of what the nightly built ([zuma ab6deaa](https://github.com/LineageOS/android_device_google_zuma/commit/ab6deaa38)). A large CP3A update series (CP3A.260905.009 and CP3A.261005.005, plus a new GKI `android14-6.1-2026-03_r24`) is **open and unmerged in Gerrit** ([Gerrit shusky](https://review.lineageos.org/q/project:LineageOS/android_device_google_shusky+branch:lineage-24.0)). That is the strongest reason to pin rather than track tips: if it merges partway, the tree and the blobs will disagree on sepolicy, VINTF and blob lists.

The alternatives are worse starting points. **Evolution-X-Devices cnb** adds a branding commit on top of Lineage shusky `bf38587` and zuma `f085107`, and turns on `BUILD_BROKEN_DUP_RULES` and `DISABLE_ARTIFACT_PATH_REQUIREMENTS` across the board. It pins `shusky-kernels` to `lineage-23.2`, and its `vendor_google_husky` cnb branch still carries BP4A (Android 16) blobs ([zuma e34d64d](https://github.com/Evolution-X-Devices/device_google_zuma/commit/e34d64d05), [shusky 297e0a8](https://github.com/Evolution-X-Devices/device_google_shusky/commit/297e0a899)). **crDroid 17.0** is the most GMS-aware fork. It enlarges partition reserves for GApps, removes the GlanceableHub overlays and supports custom AVB keys. However, it is missing Lineage's commits from August through October, and it replaces Lineage's `build_kernel` layout (`shusky-kernels/6.1`) with a flat prebuilt directory, so its device trees cannot be mixed with Lineage's kernels repo ([crDroid zuma commits](https://github.com/crdroidandroid/android_device_google_zuma/commits/17.0)). **RisingOS Revived** has shipped an Android 17 husky GApps build. It relies on blanket `BUILD_BROKEN_*` flags, and its one instructive lesson is about makefile placement (see below) ([Rising 8f6a467](https://github.com/RisingOS-Revived-devices/android_device_google_shusky/commit/8f6a46771)). AxionOS, Infinity-X, MistOS and AICP have no Android 17 husky branch. The PixelOS, Matrixx and LMODroid repos returned 404 ([Axion](https://github.com/AxionAOSP-devices/android_device_google_shusky/branches), [Infinity-X](https://github.com/Infinity-X-Devices/device_google_shusky/branches)).

DerpFest's own 16.2 trees are still useful, but as a **list of fixes to port**, not as code to sync. In particular, adi8900's zuma commit "Rename conflicting overlays with vendor/gms" and his vendor commit "husky: Drop duplicated apps" show DerpFest's preferred style: rename or narrowly remove, rather than delete wholesale ([zuma c2a2f62](https://github.com/DerpFest-Devices/device_google_zuma/commit/c2a2f62.diff), [vendor 16.2 history](https://github.com/DerpFest-Devices/vendor_google_husky/commits/16.2)).

## The recommended local manifest pins five projects to the 2026-10-16 nightly

The DerpFest 17 manifest is LineageOS's `lineage-24.0` manifest on `android-17.0.0_r1`. It keeps LineageOS `hardware/google/pixel`, swaps in DerpFest forks for `hardware/google/pixel-sepolicy` and `device/lineage/sepolicy`, and inherits `hardware/google/{camera,interfaces,av}` from Lineage's `default.xml` ([DerpFest manifest 17](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_manifest/tree/17), [Lineage default.xml](https://github.com/LineageOS/android/blob/lineage-24.0/default.xml)). Nothing under `hardware/google` needs to be added. `device/google/gs-common` must **not** be added: it has no lineage-24.0 branch, and zuma dropped the dependency on 2026-06-28 ([gs-common branches](https://github.com/LineageOS/android_device_google_gs-common/branches)). The dependency chain is husky → shusky → {zuma, shusky-kernels}. The vendor repo sits outside `lineage.dependencies` because official builds use private blobs ([husky lineage.dependencies](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LineageOS/android_device_google_husky/lineage-24.0/lineage.dependencies), [shusky lineage.dependencies](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LineageOS/android_device_google_shusky/lineage-24.0/lineage.dependencies)).

Save this as `.repo/local_manifests/husky.xml`:

```xml
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!-- Pixel 8 Pro (husky) for DerpFest 17: LineageOS lineage-24.0 trees + TheMuppets blobs,
     pinned to the official lineage-24.0-20261016 husky nightly (CP2A.260805.005). -->
<manifest>
  <remote name="lineage-gh" fetch="https://github.com/LineageOS" />
  <remote name="muppets"    fetch="https://github.com/TheMuppets" />

  <project path="device/google/husky"          name="android_device_google_husky"
           remote="lineage-gh" upstream="lineage-24.0"
           revision="58fde14a7aebbf9608f9cdc0cfb1d0cec4016eb9" />
  <project path="device/google/shusky"         name="android_device_google_shusky"
           remote="lineage-gh" upstream="lineage-24.0"
           revision="bf3858753d35531e70f79c6dca18718092be289d" />
  <project path="device/google/zuma"           name="android_device_google_zuma"
           remote="lineage-gh" upstream="lineage-24.0"
           revision="f085107fa7d119210764a83ecab92d5dbff1cbef" />
  <project path="device/google/shusky-kernels" name="android_device_google_shusky-kernels"
           remote="lineage-gh" upstream="lineage-24.0" clone-depth="1"
           revision="cd4a90f656c18677b6171cef35c5b5573ed537ca" />
  <project path="vendor/google/husky"          name="proprietary_vendor_google_husky"
           remote="muppets" upstream="lineage-24.0" clone-depth="1"
           revision="7085ddc01630fdfed802c5900db1b1b6a42cd90c" />
</manifest>
```

The first four SHAs are taken directly from the official nightly's build manifest. The Muppets SHA is the current lineage-24.0 tip (2026-08-21, "Mark NowPlayingPrebuilt.apk as PRESIGNED"). It carries CP2A.260805.005 blobs, which match the fingerprint in `lineage_husky.mk` ([Muppets repo](https://github.com/TheMuppets/proprietary_vendor_google_husky/tree/lineage-24.0), [lineage_husky.mk](https://github.com/LineageOS/android_device_google_shusky/blob/lineage-24.0/lineage_husky.mk)). Leave shusky and zuma as full clones so that `git am --3way` has history to work with. Three sync requirements are easy to miss. **First, git-lfs is mandatory.** The Muppets repo stores `radio/*.img`, the camera HAL APEX and the AiAi APKs on a non-GitHub LFS server (`lfs.undocumented.software`). Without LFS, `Android.mk`'s `add-radio-file-sha1-checked` entries fail on the 130-byte pointer files, so init with `--git-lfs` as DerpFest's README already does, and run `git lfs pull` in `vendor/google/husky` ([.lfsconfig](https://github.com/TheMuppets/proprietary_vendor_google_husky/blob/lineage-24.0/.lfsconfig), [Android.mk](https://github.com/TheMuppets/proprietary_vendor_google_husky/blob/lineage-24.0/Android.mk)). **Second, the kernel is not in the manifest.** `lunch` calls `build_kernel` when `TARGET_KERNEL_PLATFORM_SOURCE` is set. DerpFest's `build_kernel` does a nested `repo init` of `LineageOS/android_kernel_google_gs-6.1_manifest`. Its branch defaults to `KERNEL_MANIFEST_BRANCH=lineage-24.0` rather than being derived from the ROM version, so DerpFest's `DERPFEST_VERSION := 17` does not break it ([DerpFest envsetup.sh](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_vendor_derpfest/blob/17/build/envsetup.sh), [DerpFest lunch hook](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_build/blob/17/envsetup.sh)). That sync follows the branch tip (today: GKI `android14-6.1-2025-12_r30`, as in the nightly). After the first successful kernel build, record `repo manifest -r` from `out-kernel/google/gs-6.1`, then rebuild with `SKIP_KERNEL_SYNC=1` so the open r24 kernel bump cannot land unannounced ([gs-6.1 manifest](https://github.com/LineageOS/android_kernel_google_gs-6.1_manifest/blob/lineage-24.0/default.xml)). Keep the recorded manifest anyway, because it is your GPL source offer. **Third, move every pin together.** When the CP3A series merges, shusky, zuma, `shusky-kernels`, the Muppets blobs and the kernel manifest must all move in the same commit to your local manifest.

The newer tips (shusky `21e0f8219cd96a19a2801dd8c4247225d310a084`, zuma `ab6deaa38a19893fe31c2074f1ec3b1ff5973b34`) are a deliberate second step. Take them only if you choose Google face unlock, as covered in the next section. The face APEX they rely on is already in the pinned Muppets blobs ([Muppets apex dir](https://github.com/TheMuppets/proprietary_vendor_google_husky/tree/lineage-24.0/proprietary/vendor/apex)).

## Five DerpFest-specific edits make the Lineage product build as `derp`

DerpFest still uses Lineage's product naming. `check_product()` strips `lineage_` to find the device, so **keep `PRODUCT_NAME := lineage_husky`** and the `vendor/lineage/config/common_full_phone.mk` inherit. Build with `lunch lineage_husky-cp2a-user && m derp`, or `derpfest husky`. DerpFest ships the same `cp2a` release config ([DerpFest README](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_manifest/tree/17)). The output is `DerpFest-v17-YYYYMMDD-husky-Community-Stable.zip` plus `husky.json` ([version.mk](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_vendor_derpfest/blob/17/config/version.mk)). Evolution's knobs (`TARGET_HAS_UDFPS`, `TARGET_ENABLE_BLUR`, `TARGET_INCLUDE_BOOT_ANIMATIONS`) do nothing in DerpFest. Lineage already sets the 1344×2992 boot-animation size that DerpFest 16.2 used ([DerpFest 16.2 lineage_husky.mk](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DerpFest-Devices/device_google_shusky/16.2/lineage_husky.mk)).

| # | File | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | `shusky/lineage_husky.mk` | `PRODUCT_BUILD_IGNORE_APEX_CONTRIBUTION_CONTENTS := true` | `derpfest.mk` forces `WITH_GMS := true`, and gms-17 adds `com.google.android.extservices` to PRODUCT_PACKAGES. That disables AOSP's "AOSP product, ignore Google apex contributions" auto-detection in `product_config.mk`, so Soong then expects Google mainline prebuilts the tree doesn't have. This is the same edit your DerpFest kodiak port needed, and the cause is the ROM, not the device ([derpfest.mk](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_vendor_derpfest/blob/17/config/derpfest.mk), [gms-17 common-vendor.mk](https://gitlab.com/pixelos-aosp/proprietary_vendor_pixel_gms-17/-/blob/seventeen/common/common-vendor.mk)) |
| D2 | `shusky/device-husky.mk` | `TARGET_DOES_NOT_SUPPORT_GOOGLE_BATTERY := false` | gms.mk defaults this to `true` and then installs `TurboAdapter_NoBatt`. husky has google_battery ([gms.mk](https://gitlab.com/pixelos-aosp/proprietary_vendor_pixel_gms-17/-/blob/seventeen/products/gms.mk)) |
| D3 | `shusky/device-husky.mk` | Face decision: leave `TARGET_FACE_UNLOCK_SUPPORTED` at its default with the pinned trees, **or** set it `:= false` and move to the face-enabled tips | DerpFest defaults to ParanoidSense on 64-bit and otherwise installs `SettingsGoogleFutureFaceEnroll`. Three things are deliberately left out. `PRODUCT_ARTIFACT_PATH_REQUIREMENT_ALLOWED_LIST` is unnecessary because DerpFest's `BoardConfigSoong.mk` sets `DISABLE_ARTIFACT_PATH_REQUIREMENTS := true` globally, and `common_mobile.mk` already allow-lists the gms-17 `/system` apps ([BoardConfigSoong.mk](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_vendor_derpfest/blob/17/config/BoardConfigSoong.mk), [common_mobile.mk](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/android_vendor_derpfest/blob/17/config/common_mobile.mk)). `BUILD_BROKEN_DUP_RULES` is also left out. Evolution and RisingOS set it, but it turns real collisions into silent first-wins file drops, which is the kind of defect an untested release can't afford. Finally, the kodiak-style `--flags 3` lines on `vbmeta_system`/`vbmeta_vendor` are optional. Lineage zuma already sets the top-level flag, and AVB honours flags only from the top-level vbmeta, so add them only to match kodiak if you sign with the custom AVB key from `lineage-priv`. Kodiak's third DerpFest edit, commenting out `TrafficLightFaceOverlay`, has no husky counterpart: the husky blob list has no such entry. If any `PRODUCT_BROKEN_*` override becomes necessary, put it in `device-husky.mk`. RisingOS hit a "read only" error from putting `PRODUCT_BROKEN_VERIFY_USES_LIBRARIES` in BoardConfig, and its fix was to move it into the product makefile ([Rising 8f6a467](https://github.com/RisingOS-Revived-devices/android_device_google_shusky/commit/8f6a46771)).

## Four of nine Evolution patches survive the move to PixelOS gms-17

The Evolution patches in `/Users/densond/Developer/ExpressiveOS/evolution-husky/patches/` were written against Evolution's `vendor/gms` and `vendor/pixel-style`. DerpFest's collision surface is different. gms-17 suffixes every RRO overlay with `_GMS`, in both the module name and the package name (`GoogleConfigOverlay_GMS` → `com.google.android.overlay.googleconfig_gms`). It ships no GlanceableHub overlays and no Now Playing files, and it installs its sysconfig XMLs through PRODUCT_COPY_FILES rather than as modules ([overlays.mk](https://gitlab.com/pixelos-aosp/proprietary_vendor_pixel_gms-17/-/blob/seventeen/common/overlays.mk), [repo tree](https://gitlab.com/pixelos-aosp/proprietary_vendor_pixel_gms-17/-/tree/seventeen)). A name-by-name comparison of TheMuppets husky lineage-24.0 against gms-17 finds exactly **two shared Soong module names** (`DeviceIntelligenceNetworkPrebuiltAstrea` and `AICorePrebuilt-aicore_20260302.01_RC00`), both installing to the same `product/priv-app/` path. It also finds **14 shared copy-file destinations** (`google-hiddenapi-package-whitelist.xml`, `nexus.xml` and twelve `pixel_experience_*` files). ## Offline gates replace the `.device-ok` marker for an unbooted release

Your Evolution `husky-release-pack.sh` refuses to pack without a `.device-ok` marker written after on-device checks. For DerpFest, replace that marker with an `.offline-ok` marker produced by a script, and say plainly in the release post that the build has not been booted. The gate should fail the release if any check fails.

**Build log and outputs.** Build without `BUILD_BROKEN_DUP_RULES`. Grep the Kati/Soong logs for "overriding commands", "ignoring", and duplicate-destination notices from `product_copy_files_ignored`. Confirm that `product/etc/default-permissions/` contains both `default-permissions.xml` and `default-permissions-zuma.xml`, and that `nowplaying_app_26q1.xml` and `default-permissions_nowplaying.xml` are present.

**Images and firmware.** Check that `boot.img`, `dtbo.img`, `vendor_kernel_boot.img`, `init_boot.img` and `vendor_boot.img` exist, matching the image set in Lineage's nightly ([download API](https://download.lineageos.org/api/v2/devices/husky/builds)). Confirm the kernel version string is 6.1, and that the `.ko` count in `vendor_dlkm`/`vendor_kernel_boot` matches the `modules.load` lists. Unpack `payload.bin` and confirm all 11 firmware partitions are present at their real sizes (for example, `modem.img` is 112,988,160 B, not a pointer) ([.gitattributes](https://github.com/TheMuppets/proprietary_vendor_google_husky/blob/lineage-24.0/.gitattributes)). Verify that the firmware version is **at least current stock**. Pixel bootloaders refuse downgrades, and the Muppets radio files are CP2A, while your Evolution builds already ship CP3A.261005.005 firmware through `update-firmware.sh` and verify payload firmware hashes against it (`/Users/densond/Developer/ExpressiveOS/evolution-husky/README.md`).

**Policy and compatibility.** Generalise the gpuflag lesson. For every `seclabel` and `exec*` domain referenced in `vendor/etc/init/*.rc`, confirm the type exists in the built `precompiled_sepolicy` (for example with `seinfo -t`), and check that every `/vendor/bin` service has a file context. Run `checkvintf` / `check_target_files_vintf.py` on target-files, and `validate_target_files.py` with AVB verification.

**Identity and signing.** Check the props: `ro.build.type=user`, `ro.derpfest.releasetype=Community`, a vendor fingerprint of `CP2A.260805.005`, and a single face provider (either `ro.face.sense_service` or the vendor face APEX path, never both). Check the OTA signature against your own `otakey` with `check_ota_package_signature.py`.

**Differential against a device-tested build.** Extract Lineage's `lineage-24.0-20261016` husky payload. The vendor, vendor_dlkm, odm and kernel partitions should be essentially identical to yours, because the trees and blobs are the same pins. Every file-list or prop difference should trace back to a patch in your series (E8 removes gpuflag, for example). This comparison against users' real boots is the closest substitute for a phone.

Publishing has its own constraints. DerpFest's device list still shows husky as **Active under Adrian Szymański**, with an Official 16.2 build of 2026-06-12, and DerpFest 17 Official builds already exist for other devices. An official husky 17 build may therefore appear, and it is courteous to tell @Adi20_01 before posting ([device-info.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DerpFest-AOSP/Updater-Stuff/master/info/device-info.json), [SourceForge husky](https://sourceforge.net/projects/derpfest/files/husky/)). Ship as `Community` (D5) and use no official DerpFest branding. Beware `DERPFEST_BETA`, whose logic is inverted: `false` produces "Beta". Do not publish the auto-generated `husky.json` as is. `generate_json_build_info.sh` writes a URL under DerpFest's official SourceForge project, and the in-ROM Updater polls `DerpFest-AOSP/Updater-Stuff/husky.json`. That file currently points at the official 16.2 zip and will later point at an official build signed with keys that don't match yours ([husky.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DerpFest-AOSP/Updater-Stuff/master/husky.json)). Override `updater_server_url` through a device overlay, or remove the Updater, and host your own JSON. Credit LineageOS (Michael Bestas), TheMuppets and adi8900's ported commits, and link the recorded kernel manifest to meet the GPL.

## Conclusion

The DerpFest port is easier than the Evolution one in one respect and harder in another. It is easier because PixelOS gms-17 was evidently built to coexist with Lineage Pixel trees: its `_GMS` suffixes and the global artifact-path opt-out remove the two largest Evolution patches. It is harder because the remaining conflicts are mostly silent. A copy-file first-wins drop and a runtime init abort both leave the build green, and the ParanoidSense-versus-Pixel-face choice is a product decision that the build system will not make for you. With no phone available, the useful discipline is to treat LineageOS's device-tested nightly as the reference. Pin exactly to it, make each divergence a named patch, and gate the release on a diff that explains every difference.

Two open questions should be settled before the first public post. The first is whether `TurboAdapter_NoBatt` actually `overrides` gms `TurboAdapter` once D2 is set. The second is whether DerpFest's forked `pixel-sepolicy` adds anything that changes the gpuflag picture. Both can be answered offline from the built images, so they belong in the gate script rather than in a later bug report.
