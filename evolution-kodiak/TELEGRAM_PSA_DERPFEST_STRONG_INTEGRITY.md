📢 **PSA: DerpFest and Play Integrity STRONG**

DerpFest builds will not get support for **MEETS_STRONG_INTEGRITY**.

**Why:** my attempts to make STRONG available on DerpFest left my Pixel 11 Pro XL unbootable. I'm not shipping something that can do that to your phone, so DerpFest stays without it.

**What this means**
• DerpFest won't pass STRONG, and requests or bug reports about STRONG on DerpFest will be closed.
• Please don't try to force it on DerpFest yourself: the same attempts are what broke booting here.
• Everything else in DerpFest is unaffected.

**Need STRONG?** The Evolution X builds for the Pixel 11 series support it today:
