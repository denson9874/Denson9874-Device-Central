# ExpressiveOS {{VERSION}} for the {{MODEL}} — Golden Harvest

ExpressiveOS {{VERSION}} is a small update to 1.1.1: Google's on-device AI features can download their models again, and you can pick Pixel Launcher as your home screen in Expressive Studio.
On 1.1.1, it's a small update made just for your build: open the Updater and install it, and your apps and data stay. On 1.1 or 1.0, the Updater offers the full update, and your apps and data stay too. Coming from stock Android or another ROM, installing it wipes the phone, so back up first.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

#### 1. 🧠 On-device AI downloads work again
- **Before**: a Google config file in ExpressiveOS let only the Now Playing app connect to Private Compute Services, the part of Android that downloads models for Google's on-device features. Android turned everything else away, so AICore, Gboard and Android System Intelligence couldn't fetch their models or updates.
@husky - **Now**: they connect again, so Gemini Nano downloads its model and the features built on it start working. Now Playing keeps recognizing songs as before.

- Thanks to Logan in the Expressive Group for the log that found it.

#### 2. 🏠 Pixel Launcher, if you want it
- **Before**: Expressive Launcher was your only home screen.
- **Now**: Expressive Studio > Look & feel > Home screen > Launcher switches between Expressive Launcher and Pixel Launcher. The phone restarts once, and the launcher you picked runs both your home screen and recent apps. Switch back the same way. Expressive Launcher stays the default.
- If you use a navigation bar style, pick it again after switching launchers.

### 💬 Community Notes

- **Who it's for**: the {{MODEL}} ({{DEVICE}}) only. Install the build made for your exact phone.
- **Updating from 1.1.1, 1.1 or 1.0**: open the Updater and install 1.1.2; your apps and data stay. If you added root yourself, an update replaces the boot images, so add it again afterwards.
- **Installing fresh**: ExpressiveOS's packages don't carry the bootloader or modem. Flash Google's latest Android 17 factory image for your phone first, then ExpressiveOS, with the web installer or fastboot.
