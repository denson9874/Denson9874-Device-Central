### 📣 Heads-Up: ExpressiveOS 2.0 Coming in December (Clean Install Required)

- **Install 1.3.1 normally today**: `{{VERSION}}` updates your current `1.x` install in place without wiping anything.
- **Why December's 2.0 update will need a clean install**: next month's **ExpressiveOS 2.0** is rebuilt from the ground up directly on pure Android 17 AOSP alongside Google's December security update, on its own `2.0` update channel. Because the underlying OS foundation changes, **moving from 1.3.1 (or any 1.x build) to 2.0 in December will require a one-time clean install that wipes your phone**.
- **No surprise auto-update**: `1.3.1` stays on the `1.x` channel and will never force an automatic wipe. Take your time backing up your photos, chats and app data before clean-flashing `2.0` via the web installer or fastboot once December lands — all `2.x` updates after `2.0` will install in place through the Updater as usual.

### ✨ What's New & Improved

#### 1. 💬 RCS messaging & carrier registration
- **Before**: Google Messages could stay stuck on *"Setting up…"* or *"Connecting…"* after setup or an update because carrier IMS and key-registration services weren't in the default target list, and P-384 / P-521 ECDSA keys failed signature generation.
- **Now**: Google Messages and Android's carrier IMS and key-provisioning services are configured automatically (migrating existing 1.3 installs on first boot), P-256 / P-384 / P-521 ECDSA signing works natively, and kernel/boot property reporting is normalized cleanly.

#### 2. 🚀 Expressive Launcher 5.1.9 (ahead-of-time compiled)
- **Before**: `1.3` shipped Expressive Launcher `5.1.3` without build-time native compilation, stock themed-icon support, drawer sorting, or a lock on Hidden apps.
- **Now**: Expressive Launcher `5.1.9` is ahead-of-time (`speed`) compiled into `35 MB` of native Arm64 code during the build for noticeably snappier cold starts, drawer scrolling and Recents gestures right after boot, and packs every upgrade from `5.1.4` through `5.1.9`:
  - **Themed icons without an icon pack**: **Settings → Icons & appearance → Icon style → Themed icons** works with apps' built-in Android 13+ monochrome icons — no icon pack needed.
  - **Sort the app drawer & lock Hidden apps**: sort by **Alphabetical**, **Newest installed first** or **Most used first**, and require your fingerprint, face or screen lock every time **Hidden apps** opens.
  - **Photo icons, shortcut badges & Create My Widget**: frame any photo into an app icon (**From an image**), toggle **Show app badge on shortcuts**, and tap **Create** at the top of the widget picker on Pixels with Create My Widget.
  - **Wallpaper & Backup weather**: full-width scrolling across 2–3 home pages, centered single-page wallpapers, and optional **Backup weather** in At a Glance following your Regional temperature unit (°C / °F).
  - **Stability & launcher switch**: Home respects a third-party default launcher, a hidden status bar clock stays hidden across reboots, Expressive Studio's Pixel Launcher switch reads its boot property cleanly, and Recents cards guard against recycled-bitmap crashes.
@yogi   - **Foldables**: keeps all five inner-display dock slots aligned and rebuilds cover and inner navigation/taskbar bars cleanly across fold and unfold transitions.

#### 3. 🎨 Expressive Studio & Google Photos defaults
- **Before**: importing an XML configuration file opened Android's generic Recents picker and rejected Base64-encoded files, and Google Photos defaulted to legacy Pixel XL compatibility even when the Studio toggle looked off — hiding native Pixel camera and editing features.
- **Now**: the file picker opens straight to **Downloads** and accepts both plain XML and Base64-encoded files, and Google Photos compatibility defaults to **off** in both Studio and the framework so native Pixel features work out of the box (you can still turn it on anytime in **Expressive Studio → Miscellaneous → Spoofing hub**).
